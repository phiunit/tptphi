The Editor's Chair

The loop: YOU draft → your editor gives notes → YOU revise. The editor never touches your writing.

Step 1 — Draft It Yourself

Write 3–4 sentences: the About Me for a channel or profile page you'd actually make. Target = your taste file. No AI, no partner.

Step 2 — The Editor's Notes

Give your editor — a partner playing the AI, or a real AI — your draft AND taste file. Rules: ONLY the taste file, exactly 2 notes, no rewriting.

Note 1 — points at Taste File section # _____ :

Note 2 — points at Taste File section # _____ :

Step 3 — Revise It Yourself

Rewrite your draft. Star (*) every change you made.

Editor's Loop Checklist — the loop, done right✓
I wrote the first draft myself — no AI, no copying.
Each note points at a real taste-file section and is specific enough to act on — not "make it better."
I rewrote it myself and starred what changed.
