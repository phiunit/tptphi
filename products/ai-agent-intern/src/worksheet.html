Run the Quest

Name: ________________________ Class: ________ Date: ________

You're the party leader now. An AI agent is a party member — capable, tireless, EXTREMELY literal. It does what the quest card SAYS, never what you meant. Leaders don't redo the squad's work; they review it: QUEST CARD → ROLES → CHECK THE LOOT → SHIP IT or RUN IT BACK.

Step 1 — Pick Your Quest (team of 3)

Circle one:  GAME NIGHT PAGE — a one-page flyer that gets grades 6–8 to show up  ·  ZINE ISSUE #1 — the plan for your zine's first issue

Step 2 — Write the Quest Card (ONE per team — everyone copies it)

GOAL
What does winning mean?
WHAT WE KNOW
Who is it for? What do we already have?
RULES OF THE RUN
Limits: length, tone, deadline
DONE LOOKS LIKE
The exact thing handed back

Step 3 — Role Cards (take one each)

Planner
Turn the quest card into a numbered step list with a time guess per step. If the card doesn't answer something, don't guess — write "NOT ON THE CARD."
Scout
List every fact the quest needs (times, places, names, rules). Mark each ✓ verified (say where) or ? needs checking. No guesses dressed as facts.
Critic
Write 3 specific upgrades tied to the audience on the card. "Make it better" is banned — name the exact change and why.

Squad rules: work SILENTLY, from the card alone — agents can't read minds. Write your output on your own paper and hand it to your leader.
