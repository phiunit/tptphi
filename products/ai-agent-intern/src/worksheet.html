You're the boss now. An AI agent is a capable, tireless, EXTREMELY literal intern — it does exactly what the brief says, never what you meant. Bosses don't do the work twice. They run the manager's loop: BRIEF it → split the ROLES → CHECK the work → SHIP or SEND BACK.

Step 1 — Pick Your Mission (team of 3)

Circle one:  GAME NIGHT PAGE — a one-page flyer that gets grades 6–8 to show up  ·  ZINE ISSUE #1 — the plan for your class zine's first issue

Step 2 — Write the Brief (ONE per team — everyone copies it)

PartYour team's brief
GOAL
What does done look like?
CONTEXT
Who is it for? What already exists?
CONSTRAINTS
Limits: length, tone, rules, deadline
DELIVERABLE
The exact thing handed back

Step 3 — Role Cards (take one each)

Planner
Turn the brief into a numbered step list, with a time guess for each step. If the brief doesn't answer something, don't guess — write "NOT IN BRIEF" and flag it.
Researcher
List every fact the project needs (times, places, names, rules). Mark each one ✓ verified (say where) or ? needs checking. No guesses dressed up as facts.
Critic
Write 3 specific upgrades tied to the audience the brief names. "Make it better" is banned — name the exact change and why the audience would care.

Agent rules: work SILENTLY, from the brief alone — no questions, agents can't read minds. Write your output on your own paper and hand it to your boss.
