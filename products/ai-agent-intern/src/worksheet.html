Run the Quest

Name:  Class:  Date:

Everyone is a party member AND a leader. Steps 1–2 you're the leader; in Step 3 you switch and play an AI agent for your team's card; in Step 4 you're the leader again. An AI agent does a whole job, step by step, on its own — and if your card leaves something out, the agent just guesses and keeps going. So the leader checks, using the loop: QUEST CARD → ROLES → CHECK THE LOOT → SHIP IT or RUN IT BACK.

Party = your team of 3; a party member does one job. Roles = who does which slice. Quest card = the job, written down in four parts. Loot = the finished work. SHIP IT = finished and correct, send it. RUN IT BACK = do it again.

Step 1 — Pick Your Quest (team of 3, check one)

GAME NIGHT PAGE — a one-page flyer that gets grades 6–8 to show upCLASS MAGAZINE, ISSUE #1 — the plan for the first issue of a magazine your class makes

Step 2 — Write the Quest Card (ONE per team; everyone copies it)

GOAL
What does winning mean?
WHAT WE KNOW
Who is it for, and what does the team already have?
RULES OF THE RUN
Limits: length, tone, deadline
DONE LOOKS LIKE
The exact thing handed back

Step 3 — Role Cards (take one each)

Now switch: for 8 minutes YOU play an AI agent. Do your role's job SILENTLY on a blank sheet of paper — not this worksheet. No questions. When the teacher calls 4 minutes, the Critic reads the Planner's list. At 8: pass that blank sheet one seat left; keep this worksheet — you're the leader again.

Planner
0–4 min: number the steps. If the card doesn't say, don't guess — write "NOT ON THE CARD." 4–8 min: add a time guess to each step; star the one most likely to go wrong.
Scout
List every fact the quest needs. Mark each ✓ (we know it for sure — say how) or ? (needs checking — say where).
Critic
0–4 min: write 2 things the audience on the card cares about. When the teacher calls 4 minutes, read the Planner's list and write 3 upgrades. "Make it better" is banned.
