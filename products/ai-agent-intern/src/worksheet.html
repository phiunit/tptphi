Run the Quest

Name:  Class:  Date:

Everyone is a party member AND a leader. Steps 1–2: you lead. Step 3: you play an AI agent. Step 4: you lead again. An AI agent does a whole job, step by step, on its own — and if your card leaves something out, the agent usually fills the gap with a guess and keeps going. So the leader checks, using the loop: QUEST CARD → ROLES → CHECK THE LOOT → SHIP IT or RUN IT BACK.

Party = your team of 3; a party member does one job. Roles = who does which slice. Quest card = the job, written down in four parts. Loot = the finished work. SHIP IT = finished and correct, send it. RUN IT BACK = do it again.

Step 1 — Pick Your Quest (team of 3, check one)

GAME NIGHT FLYER — a one-page flyer that gets 6th graders to show upCLASS MAGAZINE, ISSUE #1 — the plan for the first issue of a magazine your class makes

Step 2 — Write the Quest Card (ONE per team)

Decide all four parts together. One person writes them: the card-holder, whose first name comes first in ABC order. The card-holder's page 1 becomes your team's quest card.

GOAL
What does winning mean?
WHAT WE KNOW
Who is it for? What does your team have?
RULES OF THE RUN
Limits: how long, what tone, when it's due
DONE LOOKS LIKE
The exact thing handed back

Step 3 — Role Cards (take one each)

Now switch: for 8 minutes YOU play an AI agent. Roles: the card-holder is the Planner. Of the other two, the first name in ABC order is the Scout. The other is the Critic.
Seats: Sit in a triangle. The Scout sits on the Planner's left; the Critic sits on the Scout's left. The card-holder's page 1 goes face-up in the middle. Read it all you want. Ask nothing.
Work SILENTLY on a blank sheet, not this worksheet. At the 4-minute call, the Critic reads the Planner's list over their shoulder.

Planner
0–4 min: number the steps to finish the quest. If the card doesn't say, don't guess — write "NOT ON THE CARD." 4–8 min: add a time guess to each step; star the one most likely to go wrong.
Scout
0–4 min: list every fact the quest needs. Mark each ✓ (we know it for sure — say how) or ? (needs checking — say where). 4–8 min: turn every ? into the one question you'd ask, in writing.
Critic
0–4 min: write 2 things the people on the card care about, and 1 thing they'd hate. 4–8 min: read the Planner's list and write 3 upgrades. "Make it better" is banned.

Step 4 — Leader Review: Check the Loot

You're the leader again. The paper in your hands is the loot. Read it like your name is on it — because it is.

  1. At 8 minutes: Every paper moves one seat to its owner's left — the Planner's plan goes to the Scout, the Scout's questions go to the Critic, the Critic's list goes to the Planner. Keep this worksheet.
  2. In every row, quote or point to one line. Then tick ONE box: SHIP IT (finished and correct, send it) or RUN IT BACK (do it again). Then write your keep-and-fix note.
  3. Last 60 seconds: hand it back. Read your FIX line out loud: Scout first, then Critic, then Planner.
