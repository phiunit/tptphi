Run the Quest

Name:  Class:  Date:

Everyone is a party member AND a leader. Steps 1–2 you're the leader; in Step 3 you switch and play an AI agent for a teammate; in Step 4 you're the leader again. Why the leader matters: an AI agent does a multi-step job on its own — and where your card is vague, it guesses forward. So the leader checks, using the loop: QUEST CARD → ROLES → CHECK THE LOOT → SHIP IT or RUN IT BACK.

Party = your team of 3; a party member does one job. Roles = who does which slice. Quest card = the job, written down in four parts. Loot = the finished work. SHIP IT = good to go. RUN IT BACK = do it again.

Step 1 — Pick Your Quest (team of 3, tick one)

GAME NIGHT PAGE — a one-page flyer that gets grades 6–8 to show upCLASS MAGAZINE, ISSUE #1 — the plan for the first issue of a magazine your class makes

Step 2 — Write the Quest Card (ONE per team; everyone copies it)

GOAL
What does winning mean?
WHAT WE KNOW
Who is it for, and what does the team already have?
RULES OF THE RUN
Limits: length, tone, deadline
DONE LOOKS LIKE
The exact thing handed back

Step 3 — Role Cards (take one each)

Now switch: for 8 minutes YOU play an AI agent. Take one role card and do that job SILENTLY, from the card alone, on your own paper. Then pass your paper one seat left — in Step 4 you're the leader again.

Planner
Turn the card into a numbered step list with a time guess per step. Card silent on something? Don't guess — write "NOT ON THE CARD."
Scout
List every fact the quest needs. Mark each ✓ (we know it for sure — say how) or ? (needs checking — say where).
Critic
Read the card as the audience. At the 4-minute call, read the Planner's list; write 3 upgrades to that plan. "Make it better" is banned.
