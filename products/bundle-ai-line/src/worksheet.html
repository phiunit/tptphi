My AI Rules

Name:   Class:   Date:

Six lessons, six rules. Write each one for your real life, in your own words. Stuck? Look back at that lesson's sheet. P.R.O. means Purpose, Requirements, Output. Your taste file lists how you like to sound.

The genie Lesson 1

Write one P.R.O. prompt you could really use next week. Give it at least two rules.

The playlist Lesson 2

Pick an app you use. Who could its playlist leave out? What would they ask for?

The fact desk Lesson 3

Write one thing you heard this week that you would check. Where would you check it?

The taste file Lesson 4

Write one line an editor should always know about how YOU sound.

The task board Lesson 5

Name one task you would never hand to a robot. Why does it need a person?

The squad Lesson 6

Your AI squad hands back its work. What is the first thing you check, and why?

The rule under all six: you start the job, AI does the middle, and you check what comes back.
