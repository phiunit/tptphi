The method

  1. UNDERLINE every claim you could check: names, dates, numbers, places, quotes. Story is a video? Write three things it shows or says, then underline those.
  2. MARK them. Write C next to claims to CHECK. Write S next to SAFE ones everyone knows.
  3. LEAVE the story. Name a second source for each C. A second source is NOT where you first saw it.
  4. VERDICT. Check one stamp. Give your evidence.
