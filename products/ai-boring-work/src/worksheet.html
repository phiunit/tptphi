Name: ________________________ Class: ________ Date: ________

You run Big Dream Studio — a small recording studio. Nia owns the place and coaches the artists. Ravi runs the mixing boards. Jaylen runs the front desk. There's too much to do, so the crew just got an AI assistant. Your job: decide what it does — and what it never touches.

  • AUTOMATE — boring and repeating. Hand it to AI.
  • AUGMENT — a human makes the call, AI helps out.
  • KEEP HUMAN — coaching, taste, being there for someone. Humans only.
