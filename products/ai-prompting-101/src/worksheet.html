Cursed Wish Repair Shop

Name:   Class:   Date:

The genie's one rule: it grants exactly what you SAY — never what you MEANT. A cursed wish is one the genie can twist. A genie-proof wish can't be twisted. Genie-proof every wish with all three P.R.O. parts:
  • P — Purpose: what you want + who it's for
  • R — Requirements: the rules — length, tone, format, what to include or leave out
  • O — Output: what the finished answer should look like (a list? a table? a paragraph?)

2. Genies are tricksters: AI can grant your wish with made-up facts — and sound totally sure. Here is the genie's answer to one class's repaired wish (about black holes):

Genie: Here are facts for your black hole poster. A black hole is a place in space where gravity pulls so hard that even light can't escape. Scientists took the first-ever photo of a black hole in 2009, and the biggest black holes sit in the middle of galaxies — including ours.

Which sentence would you double-check, and where — a real place (a textbook, a science website, a librarian…)?

Be real with yourself: If the genie does your homework, your name is on a wish you never earned — and you'd know it. Use AI to think WITH you, not FOR you.
