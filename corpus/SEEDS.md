# SEEDS.md: product ideas mined from the slipbox

Refreshed 2026-09-01. Grades 6 to 8 only. Already-cataloged products (prompting 101, fact-check lab,
vibe coding first app, pay stub, risk/EV, streamer budget, lean canvas, copywriting bars, personal
brand, paper prototype, game design doc, balance math) were excluded to avoid duplicates.
Format: `- [line] Working title: one-line concept (source: <filename>)`

- [entrepreneur] Cook or Chef?: solve one design challenge twice, once by remixing an existing model and once from first principles, then compare (source: `First Principles of Hip Hop.md`)
- [finance] Find the Arb: buy-low/sell-high simulation, then students find a real non-money arbitrage in their own school world and pitch the trade (source: `Investing & Trading/001 - Finding the arb trade (knowledge arbitrage).md`)
- [finance] Your Favorite Creator's Favorite Creator: research protocol for tracing any interest back to its source experts, three hops deep (source: `Investing & Trading/008 - How I find alpha (early, top-people, contrarian, meta-awareness).md`)
- [finance] Eight Kinds of Capital: students inventory their own social, cultural, brand, and knowledge capital, then map one conversion into another (source: `Capital/001 - Other forms of Capital.md`)
- [finance] Hours ≠ Dollars: worksheet on where "work more hours, earn more money" breaks, and what capital does instead (source: `Finance information to share to others/002 - Decoupling Money and Value.md`)
- [finance] Atoms vs. Bits Margin Game: teams price a live workshop against a digital download and discover which one gets cheaper as it scales (source: `Mental Models_Frameworks and such/055`)
- [finance] Two Prices, One Product: pricing lesson on why the same product costs a small nonprofit and a big company wildly different amounts (source: `Zeck-Tristan_meeting_notes_2026-06-03.docx`)
- [entrepreneur] 1 to 10,000, The Scaling Ladder: compute reach for 1-on-1 vs. class vs. train-the-teacher vs. async, then graph linear against non-linear (source: `Leverage/004b`)
- [entrepreneur] Shots on Goal Journal: a 10-attempt log where students record the recalibration after each try, not just the outcome (source: `Mental Models_Frameworks and such/034`)
- [entrepreneur] Top 25% + Top 25% = Top 1%: students stack two interests into a made-up specialty and name the job that lives at the intersection (source: `Entrepreneurship/011`)
- [entrepreneur] Distribution Is 80%: you built it and nobody came; students plan the launch, not the product (source: `2026-06-05_morning-pages.md`, `Businesses/002a1`)
- [entrepreneur] Who's the Customer's Customer?: B2B2C mapping activity using a school as the buyer and students as the end user (source: `Strategy/008 - Our customer's customers are the students.md`)
- [entrepreneur] Ten Small Bets Board: launch several tiny projects, track momentum, then double down on the one that moves (source: `Businesses/014`)
- [entrepreneur] Skateboard, Scooter, Car: MVP lesson where teams ship a usable skateboard version of a product instead of a broken car part (source: `Strategy/005 - Teach with skateboards not cars.md`)
- [ai] Human, AI, Human, The 80/20 Verification Lab: students prompt, then audit the output and mark exactly which 10 to 20% they had to fix (source: `AI/034 - The human verification problem.md`)
- [ai] Teach the Robot Your Taste: students build a one-page style file (favorite music, slang, formatting rules), feed it to a chatbot, and A/B the before and after (source: `[LinkedIn-Carousel] 2026-08-10 - A - AI system lessons become a curriculum.md`, `voice and taste in AI.md`)
- [ai] Robots Do the Boring Work: sort the tasks in a small business into automate / augment / keep-human, and defend the line you drew (source: `2026-04-30_morning-pages.md`, `044 - 2026-04-29 - Y - O.md`)
- [ai] Manage Your Agent Like an Intern: delegation practice with an AI team, write the brief, assign roles (planner, researcher, critic), check the work, give feedback (source: `031 - 2026-04-11 - O.md`, `2026-07-28_.md`)
- [ai] High Sugar vs. Real Food, The Feed Audit: students classify their own feed as sugary content vs. content that feeds them, then reverse-engineer how the algorithm learned their taste (source: `AI/025`, `voice and taste in AI.md`)
- [games] Fun-First Game Jam: build a tiny educational game where fun is the graded top priority and the learning has to sneak in (source: `2026-06-27_.md`, `2026-06-29_.md`)
- [games] The 1% Streak Tracker: Duolingo-style creative-streak printable, pick a tiny daily rep, log 30 days, graph the compounding (source: `004 - 2026-02-10 - Y - O.md`)
- [games] Remix 2048, Music Edition: students design a themed reskin of a known simple game, specifying rules, win state, and art direction before building (source: `Darya_meeting_notes_2026-06-11.docx`)
