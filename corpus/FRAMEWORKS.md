# FRAMEWORKS.md: Phi's mental models and teaching frameworks

Mined from the slipbox 2026-09-01. Each entry: summary in Phi's terms, source note(s), and the
product line(s) it could power (ai / finance / entrepreneur / games).

## The Cook and the Chef (first principles vs. analogy)
A cook remixes recipes other people wrote; a chef knows the flavor profiles well enough to invent a new dish. He applies it to hip-hop itself: the "four elements" were the tools of the moment, not the definition. The real fundamentals are community, youth, voices of the oppressed, and making something out of nothing. Sources: `First Principles of Hip Hop.md`, `Mental Models_Frameworks and such/047`. Lines: **entrepreneur** (strong opening unit for any line).

## Creative Confidence and the Creative Myth
The Creative Myth says artists are born, not made; no-code tools exist to tear that down. Theory of change: boost a student's creative confidence and confidence in every other part of their life rises with it. This is the spine of the whole store. Sources: `Building Beats/069`, `Building Beats/070`, `042 - 2026-04-27 - Y - O.md`. Lines: all four.

## Music as the gateway, not the destination
Use what students are already obsessed with as the hook, then smuggle in the "boring" subjects: math, civics, money, life skills. Life skills start with what's hot and trending right now. Sources: `Building Beats/059`, `Building Beats/079`, `Building Beats/021`. Lines: **games / finance** (the hook mechanic for every product).

## Fun first, education second
His stated fundamental is tying entertainment and education together, and when they conflict, fun wins. The learning has to sneak in. Sources: `2026-05-26_morning-pages.md`, `2026-06-27_.md`. Lines: **games**.

## Skateboards, not cars (Minimum Viable Beatmaking)
Both tracks end at a car, but one hands you unusable car parts and the other hands you a skateboard, then a scooter, then a motorcycle. Teach with the simple browser tool because the skateboard is usable on day one. Source: `Strategy/005`. Lines: **games / entrepreneur** (MVP for kids).

## Leverage = input × multiplier = output
Leverage is big impact from little effort. Financial and labor leverage need someone's permission; media and code don't, and AI makes both cheap. "No gatekeepers. No approval process. Just you and the tools." Sources: `Leverage/003`, `Leverage/007`, `Knooowledge/002a`. Lines: **entrepreneur / ai**.

## The Scaling Ladder
Teaching 1-on-1 reaches twelve students; teaching twelve teachers reaches 1,440; building it async reaches 10,000. Saying no to a few things and doubling down is what turns linear outcomes non-linear. Sources: `Leverage/004b`, `Knooowledge/005e`. Lines: **entrepreneur / finance** (unit economics made visual).

## The arb trade / knowledge arbitrage
Buy low in one market, sell high in another, but the version he cares about is knowledge: hearing California hip-hop radio months before the songs hit Colorado. Arbitrages close, so timing is the whole game. Sources: `Investing & Trading/001`, `057 - 2026-05-20 - Y - O.md`, `030 - 2026-04-10 - Y - O.md`. Lines: **finance / entrepreneur**.

## Forms of alpha ("your favorite DJ's favorite DJ")
Alpha isn't only financial; there is trend alpha and social alpha. Repeatable methods: get there early, trace the top person in any field back to THEIR source, connect dots across fields, stay meta-aware of where you sit on the adoption curve, be willing to be contrarian. Source: `Investing & Trading/008`. Lines: **finance / entrepreneur**.

## Eight kinds of capital, and decoupling money from time
Money is one capital among many: social, cultural, brand, intellectual, physical, spiritual. The interesting move is conversion between them, and capital is what decouples earnings from hours worked. Sources: `Capital/001`, `Finance information to share to others/002`, `Connectedism.md`. Lines: **finance** (best non-cash finance unit in the vault).

## Shots on goal, not hard work
"Hard work pays off" is the wrong lesson; shots on goal pays off. Take many attempts, extract the lesson from each, recalibrate before the next. Source: `Mental Models_Frameworks and such/034`. Lines: **entrepreneur / games**.

## Specialization by intersection (25% + 25% = 1%)
Don't compete head-on; stack two unrelated fields. Top quarter in each puts you in the top 1% of the combination. His own stack was the DJ world crossed with the education world. Source: `Entrepreneurship/011`. Lines: **entrepreneur** (career/identity unit).

## Distribution is 80%
Post-launch post-mortem: he shipped a product to little fanfare and concluded that in the AI era, marketing and distribution are the harder, more important 80%. Building the thing is now the easy half. Sources: `2026-06-05_morning-pages.md`, `Businesses/002a1`. Lines: **entrepreneur**.

## The sea of unfinished beats (creative inflation)
Tools keep getting easier: record your own loops, then drag-and-drop loops, then prompt a sentence into a song. So the scarce skill shifts from making to finishing and shipping. Ideas are worthless until implemented. Sources: `2026-01-17_morning-pages.md`, `2026-01-16_morning-pages.md`, `Ideas.../006`. Lines: **ai / entrepreneur**.

## Human, AI, Human (the 80/20 verification problem)
AI gets you 80 to 90% of the way on media, code, and text; humans begin the workflow (the prompt) and end it (verification). Prompting is the meta skill that translates across every medium. Sources: `AI/034`, `AI/028`, `AI/029`. Lines: **ai** (core unit).

## AI as the editor, not the writer
Build a workflow where AI is your feedback machine, not your ghostwriter: upload your project, get a second perspective, revise yourself. The classroom-safe AI-use policy in one sentence. Sources: `AI/003`, `AI/003a`. Lines: **ai**.

## Robots do the boring work, humans do the human work
Automate the dirty and repetitive; protect mentorship, creativity, and human connection as the irreducibly human part. Sources: `2026-04-30_morning-pages.md`, `044 - 2026-04-29 - Y - O.md`. Lines: **ai**.

## Taste is the new moat
When knowledge and generation get cheap, the differentiator is taste: knowing what's good and cutting the slop. Teach the tool your taste the way a TikTok FYP learns your likes. Sources: `2026-03-24_morning-pages.md`, `voice and taste in AI.md`. Lines: **ai / games**.

## Skills = XP (build your AI like a game character)
AI "Skills" are instructions your assistant follows for a class of problem; building them is leveling a character's ability tree. Build your own rather than downloading strangers' (craft plus security hygiene). Sources: `AI/010`, `033 - 2026-04-13 - Y - O.md`. Lines: **ai / games**.

## Manage agents like interns
Give each agent a name, a role, and a quality bar; write the brief, check the work, give feedback. Managing agents is the skill of the future, and it frees humans for people time. Sources: `031 - 2026-04-11 - O.md`, `2026-07-28_.md`. Lines: **ai / entrepreneur**.

## Fast knowledge vs. slow knowledge (Lindy)
Songs and crypto are fast knowledge with a short half-life; principles and universal themes are slow knowledge that keeps paying. Skew your time toward slow. Sources: `Mental Models_Frameworks and such/013`, `Fast knowledge vs slow knowledge.md`. Lines: **ai / entrepreneur**.

## Templates as creative leverage
A producer with a template per genre never starts from scratch. Formula doesn't kill creativity; it frees mental capacity for the part that's actually unique. Source: `010 - 2026-02-17 - Y - O.md`. Lines: **ai / games**.

## Lego blocks (Zettelkasten + AI)
Every idea written down is a lego block in a cabinet. You're not trying to finish anything; you're growing the collection so it can be assembled into things you couldn't have built alone. Source: `005 - 2026-02-11 - Y - O.md`. Lines: **ai** (note-taking / study skills).

## The 1% streak (Duolingo habit design)
Duolingo's real product is retention: a daily hit of accomplishment. He copied it for writing, 200 words a day growing to 1,000. Structure any creative habit as a streak and let it compound. Sources: `004 - 2026-02-10 - Y - O.md`, `003 - 2026-02-09 - Y - O.md`. Lines: **games / entrepreneur**.

## Side projects as risk containers
A side project lets you take real risk with no real downside; run several small bets, watch which gets momentum, double down. Building Beats itself started as a nights-and-weekends side project. Sources: `Side Projects allow for risk.md`, `Businesses/014`, `Businesses/017`. Lines: **entrepreneur**.

## The classroom DJ (energy formatting)
A DJ controls a room with warmups, peaks, and breaks; so does a teacher. Lofi to bring kids in calm, harder tracks for the checked-out older kids, music under breakouts, silence as the attention-getter. Sources: `Building Beats/050`, `Mental Models_Frameworks and such/005`. Lines: teacher-facing bonus in every product.

## The three-level reflection ladder
His actual workshop debrief structure. Level 1: the experience and creative process. Level 2: relate it to real-world content, culture, news. Level 3: apply it to our own lives. A ready-made closing routine for any lesson. Sources: `WT - 2018-06 and 07.md`, `WT - 2018-08 and 2018-09.md`. Lines: all four.

## Who's the customer's customer? (B2B2C)
Schools pay, but only because they believe their students will be satisfied. The partnership survives on the end user's experience, and the offer should remove the partner's financial risk entirely. Sources: `Strategy/008`, `Sutiweyu_meeting_notes_2026-05-26.docx`. Lines: **entrepreneur**.

## Second- and third-order effects
Keep asking "and then what?" One decision cascades into cost pressure, role confusion, and new risks two steps out. Source: `Mental Models_Frameworks and such/052`. Lines: **finance / entrepreneur**.

## Curse of knowledge / availability bias
Two communication models that pair well: you forget what outsiders don't know (so translate the jargon), and people answer with whatever's at the top of their head (so build the brand until you're the first answer). Sources: `Mental Models_Frameworks and such/032`, `Mental Models_Frameworks and such/058`. Lines: **entrepreneur**.
