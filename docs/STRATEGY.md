# Store Strategy — Phi Unit Teaches the Future

## Positioning
"Future-ready lessons for the culture." The only TPT store teaching AI, money, entrepreneurship,
and game design to grades 6–8 through hip-hop, anime, and gaming — with real standards alignment
so teachers can justify the purchase to admin.

## Why teachers will buy (the compulsion levers)
1. **Standards on the cover.** Every product leads with ISTE / CCSS / CSTA / NSPFE codes. (Note: Jump$tart and CEE jointly publish the National Standards for Personal Financial Education since 2021 — they are ONE framework, not two. Citing both is double-counting one document.)
   Personal finance is required far more widely than commonly cited: CEE's 2026 survey reports
   **39 states** requiring a personal finance course (standalone or embedded); NEFE's 2025 review
   counted 29 graduation requirements, and the standalone-only count is reported as both 26 and 30
   across sources — that discrepancy is unresolved, so cite "required in most states" rather than a
   number. See docs/MARKET.md §4.
2. **No-prep.** "Print and teach" beats brilliant-but-complicated every time on TPT.
3. **Unplugged options.** Many classrooms can't guarantee devices; every tech lesson ships with
   a no-device mode.
4. **Cultural relevance that's load-bearing.** Hip-hop/anime as the pedagogy (producer = prompter),
   not clip-art.

## Launch playbook
1. Open store with **1 freebie per line** (freebies drive followers, reviews, and search rank).
2. 2 paid products per line within 30 days; bundle each line at ~40% discount once 3+ products exist.
3. Titles are search strings, not poetry: front-load keywords teachers actually type
   ("AI literacy", "financial literacy project", "game design unit").
4. First 20 reviews matter most: price low ($3–6) at launch, raise later.
5. Every PDF's last page: store link + freebie QR + "follow for new drops" (TPT followers get
   new-product notifications).

## Positioning shift (Sep 2026)
NYC — the largest US district — banned student-facing generative AI and companion chatbots for
pre-K through grade 8 for the 2026-27 year, with a 45-min/day screen cap for grades 6–8, while
~73% of educators surveyed say grades 6–8 already get AI-literacy lessons. Competitor AI products assume
live model access. Our unplugged-by-design mandate is therefore the **headline**, not a feature:
lead every AI listing with "no student AI accounts needed."

## Release timing
- **Game design line** is our least-contested market and should be promoted out of wave 2. Time the
  push to **CSEdWeek (Dec 8–14, 2026)** with an "Hour of Code with zero computers" angle.
- **Finance line** is urgent: New York now requires personal finance by end of grade 8 starting
  this school year, and its required strands include **risk management** — which `finance-risk-boss`
  sits exactly on, in a market otherwise saturated with budgeting.
- **Career/CTE exposure** is newly mandated in more states than supply reflects (Ohio grades 7–8
  from July 2026); reframe `ent-personal-brand` as career-exploration/career-plan ready.

## The moat
Speed. This factory can ship a polished, aligned product in one session. TPT sellers average
one product a month. Publish weekly, own the "future skills" niche before it's crowded.
