## 0. Evidence quality — read first

TPT does not publish its ranking formula. Everything below is labelled:

- **[TPT-official]** — stated by TPT (Seller Blog / Help Center).
- **[Seller-consensus]** — multiple independent experienced sellers/tools agree; treat as strong
  working assumption, not fact.
- **[Single-source]** — one source only. Test before betting on it.
- **[Unverified]** — could not confirm; flagged as such.

`teacherspayteachers.com` and most seller-blog domains were **unreachable from this sandbox**
(egress-blocked), so all of this comes from search-result snippets and summaries rather than from
reading pages. Two things must be done by hand in a browser before this playbook is fully live:
1. Run the keyword lists in §3 through the **TPT Keyword Finder** in the Seller Dashboard

## 1. How TPT search actually ranks (what's known)

### 1.1 The shape of it
- Every product gets a **ranking score** from an undisclosed formula; results are sorted by that
  score descending. The formula is **A/B tested and changes**. [Seller-consensus]
  https://seotpreneur.com/tpt-search-keywords/
- Factors sellers report as confirmed by testing: **reviews, recent sales, conversion rate,
  keywords, preview and thumbnails**. [Seller-consensus] (same source)
- A ranking framing used by SEO-tool vendors: **title + start of description + long-tail keyword
  relevance + cover-image click-through + buyer behavior** are the heaviest levers.
  [Single-source, vendor] https://seolumina.com/blog/tpt-seo-guide-2026-how-to-make-your-products-rank-1-on-teachers-pay-teachers
- Title edits can move rank in as little as **~15 minutes**; some changes take **48+ hours**.
  [Single-source] https://seotpreneur.com/tpt-search-keywords/

### 1.2 Field by field

| Field | What's known | Confidence + source |
|---|---|---|
| **Title** | Strongest single text signal. Keyword in the title beats the same keyword in the description. **80-character limit.** | [Seller-consensus] https://seotpreneur.com/easy-tpt-keyword-title-optimization-finder-for-teachers-pay-teachers-sellers/ · https://seotpreneur.com/tpt-search-keywords/ |
| **Description — first ~180 chars** | ~The first 180 characters render under the title next to the cover in search results ("the snippet"). TPT calls the first 1–3 sentences the snippet and says it's the chance to get buyers interested. Keyword belongs here. | [TPT-official] https://sellerblog.teacherspayteachers.com/seller-basics-tpt-product-listing-tips-to-get-more-sales/ · [Seller-consensus] https://stageworthybywidy.com/2022/03/11/complete-guide-to-your-tpt-product-listings/ |
| **Rest of description** | Matters more for **conversion** than for ranking; structure with clear wording, sections, bullets. | [Single-source, vendor] SEOLumina (above) |
| **Tags (Theme/Audience/Language)** | Reported **maximum of six**. Tags correspond to TPT search filters. | [Seller-consensus, needs re-check in the current editor] https://stageworthybywidy.com/2022/03/11/complete-guide-to-your-tpt-product-listings/ |
| **Grade tags** | TPT recommends **no more than four grades**; fewer = clearer to buyers. Filter-matched. | [TPT-official] https://sellerblog.teacherspayteachers.com/seller-basics-tpt-product-listing-tips-to-get-more-sales/ |
| **Subject / Resource Type tags** | These *are* the search filters — if a buyer filters on a subject and you're not tagged for it, you are invisible in that view. "Not subject specific" exists as a fallback tag. | [TPT-official] https://sellerblog.teacherspayteachers.com/improvements-to-tags-and-filters-on-tpt/ |
| **Standards alignment** | Buyers can check standards alignment right in the preview — align it in the listing, don't only put it in the PDF. | [TPT-official/consensus] https://sellerblog.teacherspayteachers.com/how-to-make-products-to-sell-on-tpt/ |
| **Reviews** | Products with more and higher reviews are more likely to rank near the top; review count is a good proxy for the ranking factors. **Recency-weighted decay is reported** — a recent review counts more than an old one. | [Seller-consensus] https://www.missbeccasclassroom.com/2025/01/16/teachers-pay-teachers-seo-the-ultimate-guide/ · recency claim [Single-source, vendor] JustNiches https://justniches.com/blog/tpt-seo-2026-guide |
| **Review supply reality** | **Most buyers never review**; in some popular stores **<10%** do. TPT awards buyers credits for reviews; sellers may **not** incentivize positive reviews. | [Seller-consensus] https://lindsaybowden.com/podcast/how-to-get-more-tpt-reviews/ · [TPT-official] https://help.teacherspayteachers.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042243512-Ratings-Reviews-Guidelines-for-Sellers |
| **Cover image** | Not a text signal, but drives **click-through**, and CTR feeds performance over time. | [Single-source, vendor] SEOLumina (above) |
| **Freshness** | Listings updated periodically stay more competitive, especially in seasonal categories. Buyers can also *sort* by recency. | [Single-source, vendor] SEOLumina · sort options [TPT-official] https://help.teacherspayteachers.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042882311-How-do-I-search-for-resources |
| **Followers** | Followers get notified of new products. Documented mechanism: a strong **freebie** → followers → early sales/reviews on paid items. Not a proven direct ranking input. | [TPT-official, marketing] https://sellerblog.teacherspayteachers.com/how-to-market-your-tpt-store/ |
| **Freebie halo** | Free resources give risk-free exposure to your formatting/quality and drive same-session or return purchases; TPT recommends freebies as a marketing lever. **The "free listing lifts paid ranking" claim is [Unverified].** | [TPT-official, marketing] same source |

### 1.3 Two operational rules that fall out of this
1. **One primary keyword phrase per product**, used naturally in: title, first 1–3 sentences of the
   description, and the preview file. TPT's own SEO post says using too many keywords hurts.
   [TPT-official] https://sellerblog.teacherspayteachers.com/tpt-seller-seo-tips/
2. **Buyers search long.** TPT advises writing for the longer, more specific phrase a buyer who'd
   love your resource would actually type. [TPT-official] (same source)

### 2.2 The patterns, abstracted

```
A. PRIMARY KEYWORD | GRADE BAND | SECONDARY KEYWORD
   "AI Literacy Worksheets | Grades 6 7 8 | Artificial Intelligence Unit"
   → pipe-delimited keyword stacking; each segment is its own searchable phrase.

B. FREE + PRIMARY KEYWORD + FORMAT + GRADE + "Sample"
   "FREE AI Literacy Activity | Artificial Intelligence Middle School Sample"
   → FREE in caps up front; "Sample" signals it's a funnel into a paid unit.

C. QUANTITY + FORMAT + TOPIC + "for Middle/High School"
   "10 Pack Video Game Design Worksheets for Middle/High School"
   → the number does the conversion work; the grade band widens the match.

D. OUTCOME + "Curriculum"/"Course" + DURATION + "for Middle School"
   "Create a Business Curriculum, 9-Week Entrepreneurship Course for Middle School"
   → duration ("9-Week", "3-Day") is a real buyer filter — it answers "does this fit my unit?"

E. TOPIC + FORMAT + "Bundle"/"Mega Bundle"
   "Entrepreneurship Activities and Projects Mega Bundle"

F. OCCASION/SEASON + FORMAT + "No Prep Lesson Plans"
   "Third Grade Emergency Sub Plans January | Hibernation No Prep Lesson Plans"
   → grade word first, season second, "No Prep" as the closing trust signal.
```

### 2.3 Our house title formula (80 chars, hard cap)

```
<PRIMARY KEYWORD PHRASE> | <Format word> | Grades 6-8 | <Differentiator>
```
Fill the **whole 80 characters** — long titles reportedly out-rank short ones
[Single-source, cross-marketplace claim, treat with care]:
https://seotpreneur.com/easy-tpt-keyword-title-optimization-finder-for-teachers-pay-teachers-sellers/
— but do not sacrifice readability; keyword stuffing is explicitly discouraged by TPT (§5).

**Slot rules**
1. **Primary keyword first**, verbatim as teachers type it (not our internal name).
2. **Format word** — one of: Lesson, Worksheets, Project, Unit, Task Cards, Escape Room,
   Bell Ringers, Doodle Notes, Sub Plans, Bundle, Posters.
3. **Grade band** — write `Grades 6-8` (and consider adding `Middle School`, which is its own
   heavily used search phrase — see §3).
4. **Differentiator** — our actual edge, and the terms are searchable:
   `No Prep`, `Print & Go`, `No Tech`, `Unplugged`, `Standards-Aligned`, `FREE`.

**Our current catalog titles, graded** (see `curriculum/catalog.yaml`):

| Current | Problem | Suggested rewrite (≤80 chars) |
|---|---|---|
| `How to Talk to AI: Prompt Writing Lesson + Activity (Grades 6-8)` | Cleverness in slot 1; "How to Talk to AI" is not a search phrase | `AI Literacy Lesson: Prompt Writing Activity \| Grades 6-8 \| FREE No Prep` |
| `Hallucination Hunters: Fact-Checking AI Like a Journalist` | No grade band, no format, no keyword | `AI Fact-Checking Lesson \| Artificial Intelligence Grades 6-8 \| No Tech` |
| `What's in the Playlist? How AI Learns + Bias Lesson` | Keyword buried past the hook | `AI Bias Lesson: How AI Learns \| Artificial Intelligence Grades 6-8` |
| `Nerf This! Game Balance Is Just Math (Ratios & Stats)` | Great hook, zero keywords | `Game Design Math Lesson: Balance, Ratios & Stats \| Grades 6-8 No Prep` |
| `AI Literacy Unit Bundle: 6 Lessons (Grades 6-8)` | Already good — closest to pattern A | `AI Literacy Unit \| 6 Lessons Grades 6-8 \| Artificial Intelligence Bundle` |

**Keep the hook — move it.** Put "Hallucination Hunters" and "Nerf This!" in the **cover art** and
the **first line of the description**, where they sell, not in the title, where they cost us the match.

## 3. High-intent search phrases, per line

### How to read these lists
- **[live path]** = I observed a live TPT search-results URL for this exact phrase
  (`teacherspayteachers.com/browse?search=<phrase>`). That proves the phrase is a real search path
  on TPT. **It does not prove volume.**
- **HV** = likely high-volume/high-competition (broad, generic, many incumbents).
- **LT** = long-tail, winnable, lower competition — where a new store should actually rank.
- Unmarked = plausible from competitor titles/copy but **unverified as a search phrase**.

**Verification protocol before you use any of these (do this, it's five minutes per product):**
1. Type the phrase into the TPT search bar. **If it autocompletes as a suggestion, it has real
   popularity with teachers** [TPT-official-adjacent seller guidance]:
   https://kristendoyle.co/podcast-episodes/my-top-3-seo-tips-for-tpt-sellers/
2. Check volume/competition in the **TPT Keyword Finder** (Seller Dashboard).
   https://sellerblog.teacherspayteachers.com/tpt-keyword-finder/
3. Skim page 1 of results. If page 1 is all 500+-review incumbents, pick the LT variant instead.

## 4. Description structure that converts

### 4.1 The snippet (first ~180 characters — the only part shown before "read more")
This is the highest-leverage prose we write. It must do **three** jobs in ~180 characters:
primary keyword, the no-prep promise, and the grade band.

**Template**
```
<Primary keyword phrase> for grades 6-8 — print and teach the same morning.
No prep, no devices needed. <Standards families>. Includes <count> student pages + answer key.
```

**Worked example (`ai-prompting-101`)**
```
AI literacy lesson for grades 6-8 — print and teach the same morning. No student AI accounts
and no devices required. ISTE + CCSS-ELA aligned. 45 minutes, worksheet + exit ticket included.
```
That's ~200 chars; trim to land the value inside 180. TPT calls these first 1–3 sentences the
snippet and says they're the chance to hook buyers.
[TPT-official] https://sellerblog.teacherspayteachers.com/seller-basics-tpt-product-listing-tips-to-get-more-sales/

**Rules for the snippet**
- Primary keyword in the **first six words**. Keyword placed too late loses relevance.
  [Single-source, vendor] https://seolumina.com/blog/how-to-write-tpt-product-descriptions-that-rank-and-sell
- Lead with **value/outcome**, not a contents list — TPT explicitly says explain how it makes a
  difference in a teacher's life (saves time, more engaging), not just what's inside.
  [TPT-official] (Seller Basics, above)
- Never keyword-stuff it. See §5.

### 4.2 Body order (our house structure)
1. **The teacher's problem, in one line.** ("You need an AI lesson and your students can't touch AI.")
2. **What's in the box** — bulleted, with page counts and minutes per activity.
3. **How it runs** — timings, the unplugged variant, the print instructions (B&W-safe, 1 copy/student).
4. **Standards, in full text plus code.** 3–5 real codes, verified against `docs/STANDARDS.md`.
   Put them in the **top third** of the description per our own CLAUDE.md rule, and align them in the
   listing's standards field too, since buyers can check alignment in the preview.
   [TPT-official/consensus] https://sellerblog.teacherspayteachers.com/how-to-make-products-to-sell-on-tpt/
   For finance, name the requirement plainly and sourceably (see MARKET §4) — e.g. New York's
   end-of-grade-8 personal finance requirement — but **never overstate a mandate**.
5. **Who it's for** — course names a teacher would recognize: "AI literacy, digital citizenship,
   advisory, STEM elective, CTE career exploration, consumer math."
6. **Assessment included** — exit ticket / rubric, named.
7. **Bottom of description:** related products (link the line bundle), the "follow for new drops"
   line, and the freebie pointer. Followers get new-product notifications, which is the
   documented compounding mechanism.
   [TPT-official, marketing] https://sellerblog.teacherspayteachers.com/how-to-market-your-tpt-store/

### 4.3 Description hygiene
- **Never** reuse a description across products — duplicate/generic descriptions weaken each
  listing's distinct relevance. [Single-source, vendor] SEOLumina (above)
- Sections and bullets, not walls of text — easier for buyers **and** for search systems.
  [Single-source, vendor] SEOLumina
- Put the store name **in the description**, never on the lesson pages themselves (our own
  CLAUDE.md branding rule).

## 5. Cover / thumbnail best practices (search-results legibility)

- Design **square**: 750×750 px, or 1080×1080 / 8×8 in.
  https://raerocksteaching.com/tpt-thumbnail-size-how-to-optimize-your-thumbnails-for-more-clicks-and-sales/
- **The title must be readable at thumbnail size.** Test it small and on mobile before publishing.
  (same source)
- **Bold block fonts, high contrast, no script/cursive.** Many sellers put a **solid color box
  behind the title text** so it survives any background. (same source) ·
  https://learn.maliarivera.com/blog/create-eye-catching-tpt-covers
- **Don't overcrowd.** Too much text = unreadable on mobile. (same source)
- **No low-resolution art** — pixelation makes buyers doubt the product's quality. (same source)
- The cover isn't a text ranking signal but it drives **click-through**, which feeds performance
  over time. [Single-source, vendor] SEOLumina §1.

**Our house cover spec (extends `docs/ANTI_SLOP.md`)**
1. Line 1 of the cover = the **primary keyword phrase**, 3–7 words, block caps, in a solid band.
2. Line 2 = `GRADES 6-8` + the format word.
3. A **badge**: `NO PREP` / `NO TECH NEEDED` / `PRINT & GO` — our differentiator, visible at 150 px.
4. One spot illustration from the lesson's analogy (our inline-SVG system), **not** a page-mosaic —
   a mosaic of shrunken pages is unreadable at thumbnail size.
5. Photocopy-safe palette: dark line art + one accent. Verify legibility in grayscale.
6. Preview file: show key pages, not everything; watermark it; caption pages to explain how it runs.
   https://sellerblog.teacherspayteachers.com/how-to-make-products-to-sell-on-tpt/

## 8. Things we could not verify (do not assert these)
1. **Exact ranking weights.** No source, including TPT, publishes them.
2. **That free products directly lift paid listings' rank.** Freebies verifiably drive followers and
   exposure; the ranking-lift mechanism is [Unverified].
3. **Current tag-field limits.** "Six theme tags" is seller-reported and may have changed — confirm
   in the live product editor.
4. **That 75–80-char titles out-rank shorter ones on TPT specifically.** The claim comes from a
   cross-marketplace audit framing; treat as a hypothesis and A/B it on two of our own products.
5. **Search volume for every phrase in §3.** [live path] proves the phrase exists as a search path,
   not that anyone searches it much. Keyword Finder is the only real answer.
6. **TPT's 2026 sitewide sale dates** beyond the reported Aug 25–26 back-to-school sale. TPT
   announces these itself — subscribe rather than plan on secondhand dates.
