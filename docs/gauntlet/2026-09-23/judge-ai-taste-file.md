## Verdict

This is one of the most on-voice lessons in the line. "Your For-You Page didn't guess your taste. You taught it" is Phi's own FYP-to-AI analogy (VOICE #8) and his "taste is the new moat" stance (VOICE #19) turned into a 45-minute routine. The choreography is tight: a timed swap, a turn order a sub could run, "No section, no note," "Hand the pen back." The Jaylen pancake example is the funniest, most human moment in the unit. Today it would get four stars, not five, and the lost star is in the details a careful teacher checks first. The worked example cites "Section 3 says no textbook voice," but Jaylen's own Section 3 says no such thing. The first modeled "good note" (plan + slide 5) breaks the lesson's one rule by naming no section. The student page asks for the DOK-3 note with no model note on it. The revise instruction contradicts itself, and the exemplar breaks it. The cover art shows a line crossed out with a replacement written in, which is the ghostwriting the lesson forbids. The preview hides the two pages that sell the lesson (The Editor's Chair and the Jaylen walkthrough) behind a page of standards and terms. None of this needs new pages. It is an afternoon of edits, most of them copying one fixed string across surfaces.
